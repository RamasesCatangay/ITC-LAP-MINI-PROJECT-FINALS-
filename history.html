How it started

English football has been going since the 1800s. The Football League started in 1888 and the top division was called the First Division. In 1992 the biggest clubs wanted more control and more TV money, so they left the Football League and started a new league called the Premier League.

The league started with 22 teams in 1992-93. It was cut down to 20 teams in 1995. The first ever winner was Manchester United. TV company Sky paid a lot of money to show the games, which made the league super rich.

Important Moments

  • 1992 - The first Premier League season starts
  • 1995 - League goes down to 20 teams, Blackburn Rovers win the title
  • 1999 - Manchester United win the treble
  • 2003-04 - Arsenal finish the season unbeaten (the Invincibles)
  • 2003 - Roman Abramovich buys Chelsea and they start winning titles
  • 2008 - Abu Dhabi group buys Manchester City
  • 2011-12 - Man City win the league with Aguero's last minute goal
  • 2013 - Sir Alex Ferguson retires from Man United
  • 2015-16 - Leicester City win the league (5000 to 1 odds!)
  • 2017-18 - Man City get 100 points
  • 2019-20 - Liverpool win the league for the first time
  • 2022-23 - Man City win the treble
  • 2023-24 - Man City win 4 titles in a row
  • 2024-25 - Liverpool win the league under Arne Slot
  • 2025-26 - Arsenal win the league
  • 2026 - Pep Guardiola leaves City after 10 years

Crazy Stories

  • The Invincibles: Arsenal went 49 games unbeaten in the league from 2003 to 2004.
  • Leicester 2016: Leicester were nearly relegated the year before, then won the title. Many people say it is the biggest shock in sports history.
  • Derby County 2007-08: Derby got only 11 points, the worst season ever.
  • Champions list is small: In 34 seasons only 7 different clubs have won the league.
