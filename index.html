What is the Premier League?

The Premier League is the top level (tier 1) of the English football pyramid. It is run by the Premier League company, which is owned by the clubs in it. The season goes from August to May.

How it works

  • There are 20 teams in the league
  • Every team plays every other team twice, once at home and once away
  • That means each team plays 38 games and there are 380 games in total
  • A win is 3 points, a draw is 1 point and a loss is 0 points
  • The team with the most points at the end wins the league
  • If teams have the same points, goal difference decides (goals scored minus goals conceded)
  • The 3 teams at the bottom get relegated to the Championship (tier 2)
  • 3 teams from the Championship get promoted to take their place

Why do people play for a top 4 finish?

The teams at the top of the table qualify for European competitions like the Champions League, which brings in lots of money and the best players. The lower European spots are for the Europa League and the Conference League.
