The Story of City

The early days

City began in 1880 as a church team called St. Mark's. They became Ardwick AFC in 1887 and then Manchester City in 1894. They won their first big trophy, the FA Cup, in 1904.

The good times in the 1930s and 1950s

City won the league for the first time in 1936-37. In 1956 they won the FA Cup, and goalkeeper Bert Trautmann played the end of the final with a broken neck without knowing it!

The golden era (1960s and 70s)

Under manager Joe Mercer and coach Malcolm Allison, City won the league in 1967-68, then the FA Cup in 1969, the League Cup and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1970. Legends like Colin Bell, Mike Summerbee and Francis Lee played in this team.

The bad times

After that City struggled for years. In 1998 they dropped all the way down to the third tier of English football. In 1999 they were losing 2-0 in the playoff final against Gillingham but came back with a goal from Paul Dickov in the last minutes and won on penalties. Many City fans say this was when the club turned around.

The takeover

In 2008 the club was bought by the Abu Dhabi United Group. The first big signing was Robinho. The owners spent a lot of money on players and facilities, and City became one of the richest clubs in the world. A new training ground called the Etihad Campus was also built.

Famous Moments

  • 2011 FA Cup: City beat Stoke 1-0 (Yaya Toure scored) and won their first trophy in 35 years.
  • 13 May 2012 - "93:20": On the last day of the season City needed to beat QPR to win the league. They were losing 2-1 until injury time. Edin Dzeko equalised and then Sergio Aguero scored in the 94th minute to win 3-2 and take the title from Man United on goal difference. This is the most famous moment in City history.
  • 2017-18 - The Centurions: City got 100 points, the most ever in a Premier League season. They also scored 106 goals and won 32 games.
  • 2018-19 - Domestic Treble: City won the Premier League, FA Cup and League Cup in the same year, the first English men's team to do this. They beat Liverpool to the league by just 1 point (98 to 97). Vincent Kompany scored a rocket from 30 yards against Leicester that helped them win.
  • 2022-23 - The Treble: City won the Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League. In the Champions League final in Istanbul they beat Inter Milan 1-0 with a goal from Rodri. Erling Haaland also scored 36 league goals that season, a record for a 38 game season.
  • 2023-24 - Four in a row: City won their 4th league title in a row, which no other team in English top-flight history has done.

City Legends

Sergio Aguero

Argentine striker, joined in 2011 and left in 2021. He is City's all-time top scorer with 260 goals and scored 184 in the Premier League. He is the person who scored the "93:20" goal.

Vincent Kompany

Belgian centre back and captain from 2011 to 2019. He won 4 league titles as captain and is seen as the leader of the first modern City team.

David Silva

Spanish playmaker, known as "Merlin" because of his magic on the ball. He played 10 years at City (2010 - 2020) and won 4 league titles.

Yaya Toure

Ivorian midfielder who was powerful and could score from anywhere. He scored the winner in the 2011 FA Cup final and also scored in the 2014 League Cup final.

Kevin De Bruyne

Belgian midfielder who joined in 2015 and is often named the best passer in Premier League history. He won 6 league titles and the Champions League at City before leaving in 2025.

Older legends

  • Colin Bell - nicknamed "King of the Kippax" and one of the club's greatest ever.
  • Bert Trautmann - the German goalkeeper who played on with a broken neck.
  • Mike Summerbee and Francis Lee - stars of the 1960s and 70s.
  • Shaun Goater - fans sang "feed the Goat" when City were in the lower leagues.

The Pep Guardiola Era (2016 - 2026)

Pep Guardiola became manager in 2016 and stayed for 10 years. He won 6 Premier League titles, the Champions League, and many more trophies. He changed English football with his passing style, using players like Rodri, De Bruyne, Foden and Haaland. In 2026 he left the club and the new manager is Enzo Maresca.

Erling Haaland

Haaland joined from Borussia Dortmund in 2022 and scored 36 league goals and 52 in all competitions in his first season, both records. He is one of the fastest players to ever reach 100 Premier League goals. He is now one of City's four captains.

2026-27 Season (new era!)

  • New manager: Enzo Maresca replaces Pep Guardiola. Maresca worked at City as one of Pep's assistant coaches during the 2022-23 treble season, so he knows the club.
  • New signings: Gianluigi Donnarumma (goalkeeper), Marc Guehi (centre back) and Elliot Anderson (midfielder).
  • Captains: Ruben Dias, Erling Haaland, Marc Guehi and Gianluigi Donnarumma are the four-man captain group.
  • Start: City won their first 3 league games of the season.
  • Last season: City did not win the league in either 2024-25 or 2025-26 (Liverpool and Arsenal won them), so fans want the title back.

Transfers and results change quickly, so check the official City website for the latest.

Manchester Derby

City vs United is the biggest game in Manchester. For years United were much better, but since 2011-12 City have won many more titles. Famous derbies include City's 6-1 win at Old Trafford in 2011 and the 6-3 win at the Etihad in 2013.
