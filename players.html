Legends of the Premier League

Alan Shearer

Alan Shearer is the all time top scorer in the Premier League with 260 goals. He played for Blackburn Rovers and Newcastle United. He won the league with Blackburn in 1994-95.

Thierry Henry

Thierry Henry played for Arsenal and scored 175 Premier League goals. He was part of the "Invincibles" team that went a whole season without losing. He also won the Golden Boot 4 times.

Wayne Rooney

Wayne Rooney was a striker for Manchester United and England. He scored 208 Premier League goals and won 5 league titles. He is also Man United's all time top scorer in all competitions.

Steven Gerrard

Steven Gerrard was a midfielder and captain of Liverpool. He was famous for long range shots and the 2005 Champions League comeback in Istanbul. He never won the Premier League though.

Ryan Giggs

Ryan Giggs won 13 Premier League titles with Manchester United, which is the most of any player. He also has the most assists in the league's history (162).

Eric Cantona

Eric Cantona was a French forward at Manchester United who helped start their success in the 1990s. Fans called him "King Eric".

Petr Cech

Petr Cech was Chelsea's goalkeeper and has the most clean sheets in the Premier League (202). He wore a special helmet after a head injury in 2006.

Mohamed Salah

Salah played for Liverpool and is one of the best forwards the league has seen. He scored 32 goals in a 38 game season in 2017-18, and was key to both of Liverpool's Premier League titles.

Man City legends

Sergio Aguero, Vincent Kompany, David Silva, Yaya Toure and Kevin De Bruyne all played for City. Read about them on the Man City page!

Current Stars
