The "Big Six"

People call six clubs the "Big Six" because they are the richest and most famous: Manchester City, Manchester United, Liverpool, Arsenal, Chelsea and Tottenham.

Manchester United

The most successful team in Premier League history with 13 titles, most of them under Sir Alex Ferguson who managed the club from 1986 to 2013. In 1999 they won the treble (league, FA Cup and Champions League).

Liverpool

Liverpool have won the European Cup / Champions League 6 times. They won the Premier League for the first time in 2019-20 (ending a 30 year wait for a league title) and again in 2024-25.

Arsenal

Arsenal won the league in 1997-98, 2001-02, 2003-04 and 2025-26. In 2003-04 they were the "Invincibles" because they did not lose a single game all season.

Chelsea

Chelsea became rich after Roman Abramovich bought the club in 2003. They have won 5 league titles and the Champions League in 2012 and 2021.

Tottenham

Spurs have never won the Premier League but they did win the Europa League in 2025, which ended a long wait for a trophy.

Manchester City

The best team of the last 10 years! 8 Premier League titles, the Champions League in 2023 and a treble. See the Man City page for much more.

Famous Rivalries (Derbies)

Some games are extra special because the teams really dislike each other:

  1. Manchester Derby - Man City vs Man United. The two clubs are only a few miles apart.
  2. North London Derby - Arsenal vs Tottenham.
  3. North West Derby - Manchester United vs Liverpool. The two most successful clubs in English history.
  4. Merseyside Derby - Liverpool vs Everton.
  5. Tyne-Wear Derby - Newcastle vs Sunderland.
  6. West London Derbies - Chelsea vs Fulham.
